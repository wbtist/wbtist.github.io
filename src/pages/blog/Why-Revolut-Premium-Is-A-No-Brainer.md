## Why Revolut is actually a game-changer

In this post, I want to explain why using Revolut is not just useful, but honestly one of the best, most cost-effective products available today. At least here in the UK, where I am based.

If you’re reading this, you might have come from my previous post [How to get your VPN for next to nothing](/blog/How-to-Access-My-App-with-NordVPN/). If not, you might be wondering why you should consider getting a VPN, but more on that later, with the final reveal at the bottom of the page.

### The Power of the Premium Account

If you’re willing to upgrade to a **Revolut Premium Account**, it costs around £90 a year (you can also pay monthly). The best part? You get a **NordVPN "Standard" subscription included** in that price!

Honestly, for many people, the NordVPN subscription alone makes the account worth it. But Revolut doesn't stop there.

### What else do you get?

The list of goodies you get with a Premium account is actually quite impressive. Here are a few of the bits and bobs included:

* **NordVPN Standard**
* **Perplexity Pro**
* **Uber One**
* **Tinder Plus**
* **Picsart Pro**
* ...and plenty more. You can check out the full list on their official site.

### It’s a proper bank

I’m not sure about other countries, but here in the UK, Revolut is now fully recognised as a bank. But my favourite part isn't the status, it's the security.

There is one unbeatable feature: the **Disposable Virtual Card**.

If you’re shopping on a website you haven't used before and feel a bit iffy about it, you should use this. It’s a single-use card that the Revolut app generates for you automatically every single time. Once you pay, that card "self-destructs," making it impossible for anyone to charge it again.

### Five years and counting

I’ve been using Revolut for at least five years now, and I wouldn't be without it.
