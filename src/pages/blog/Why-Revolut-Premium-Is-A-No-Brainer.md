## Why Revolut is actually a game-changer

In this post, I want to explain why using Revolut is not just useful, but honestly one of the best financial moves you can make.

If you’re reading this, you’ve probably come from my previous post [How to get your VPN for next to nothing](/blog/How-to-Access-My-App-with-NordVPN/).

### The Power of the Premium Account

If you’re willing to upgrade to a **Revolut Premium Account**, it costs roughly £80 a year at the time of writing (though you can pay monthly if you prefer). The standout feature? You get a **NordVPN "Standard" subscription included** in that price!

Honestly, for many people, the NordVPN subscription alone makes the account worth it. But Revolut doesn't stop there.

### What else do you get?

The list of "pats on the back" you get with a Premium account is actually quite impressive. Here are a few of the bits and bobs included:

* **NordVPN Standard**
* **Perplexity Pro**
* **Uber One**
* **Tinder Plus**
* **Picsart Pro**
* ...and plenty more. You can check out the full, up-to-date list on their official site linked below.

### It’s a proper bank (and it's safe!)

At least here in the UK, Revolut is now fully recognised as a bank. But my favourite part isn't the status—it's the security.

There is one unbeatable feature: the **Virtual Disposable Card**.

Whenever you’re shopping on a website you haven't used before and feel a bit iffy about it, you should use this. It’s a single-use card that the Revolut app generates for you automatically every single time. Once you pay, that card "self-destructs," making it impossible for anyone to charge it again.

### Five years and counting

I’ve been using Revolut for at least five years now, and I wouldn't be without it.
